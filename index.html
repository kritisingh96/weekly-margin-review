      Assumptions and data dictionary

      Margin floor (35%)
      Illustrative assumption, not an actual company policy. Chosen as a level that leaves room to fund one promotion and absorb freight swings while still covering selling and overhead costs. Change it in the control at the top to test other floors.
      Units
      All units are cases. Bars are 12 count boxes, drinks are 12 packs.
      Gross sales
      Cases × price per case to the retailer.
      Trade spend
      Promotional and allowance payments to the retailer, per case sold.
      Net sales
      Gross sales − trade spend.
      COGS
      Cost to make one case, including ingredients, packaging and co packing.
      Freight
      Cost to deliver one case to the retailer's warehouse. Varies by retailer.
      Gross margin
      (Net sales − COGS − freight) ÷ net sales.
      Baseline
      Average weekly cases and per case economics over up to 4 prior weeks without the promotion.
      Incremental gross profit
      Promo week gross profit (after trade) − baseline gross profit.
      Promotion ROI
      Incremental gross profit ÷ trade spend. Below 0% means the promotion lost money versus not running it.
      Breakeven lift
      Sales lift over baseline the promotion needs for gross profit to match a normal week.
      DSO, DIO, DPO
      Days sales outstanding (AR ÷ net sales × 365), days inventory outstanding (inventory ÷ COGS × 365), days payables outstanding (AP ÷ COGS × 365), using trailing 12 month totals.
      Cash conversion cycle
      DSO + DIO − DPO. The number of days cash is tied up between paying suppliers and collecting from retailers.

      How this would run in production

      Step 1PullNetSuite saved search for invoices, trade and COGS loads into the finance data platform each Monday.
      Step 2CheckData quality tests: totals tie to NetSuite, no missing SKUs, no duplicate invoices.
      Step 3FlagEvery retailer and SKU is tested against the margin floor. Only exceptions surface.
      Step 4DraftClaude writes first pass commentary and suggests the driver behind each exception.
      Step 5ReviewAnalyst edits, confirms drivers with Sales or Ops, and sends the pack.
