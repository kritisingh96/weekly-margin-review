# Weekly Margin Review

An AI-assisted weekly sales and margin review for a consumer packaged goods (CPG) snack brand, built on **sample data**.

**Live demo:** https://kritisingh96.github.io/weekly-margin-review/

![Management summary](screenshots/management_summary.png)

## What it does

- **Management Attention Required:** three takeaways a CFO can read in 15 seconds: biggest margin pressure, product needing a cost or pricing review, and the recommended action.
- **Margin floor checks:** tests every SKU and retailer against a margin floor (35%, an illustrative assumption) and lists only the exceptions, with the likely driver and a next step.
- **Promotion ROI:** compares each promo week to a baseline of up to 4 prior non promo weeks to calculate lift, incremental gross profit, ROI and breakeven lift.
- **Cash and working capital:** tracks AR, inventory, AP, DSO, DIO, DPO and the cash conversion cycle.
- **AI drafted weekly summary:** plain English commentary generated from the data, designed for analyst review before it goes out. (In this demo it is generated by rules. In production it would be drafted by an LLM such as Claude.)

Change the week or the margin floor at the top of the page and everything updates.

![Promotion ROI](screenshots/promotion_roi.png)

## Key definitions

| Term | Formula |
|---|---|
| Net sales | Gross sales minus trade spend |
| Gross margin | (Net sales minus COGS minus freight) / net sales |
| Lift | (Promo cases minus baseline cases) / baseline cases |
| Incremental gross profit | Promo week gross profit minus baseline gross profit |
| Promotion ROI | Incremental gross profit / trade spend |
| Breakeven lift | Lift needed for promo gross profit to equal a normal week |
| Cash conversion cycle | DSO + DIO minus DPO |

Units are cases. Full definitions are in the dashboard's data dictionary.

## How it would run in production

1. **Pull** invoices, trade and COGS from the ERP (for example NetSuite) into a finance data platform
2. **Check** data quality: totals tie to the ERP, no missing SKUs, no duplicate invoices
3. **Flag** items below the margin floor so only exceptions surface
4. **Draft** commentary with AI
5. **Review** by an analyst before it is shared

## Data

`data/sample_sales_data.csv` holds the sample dataset: 6 SKUs x 4 retailers x 8 weeks. All products, retailers and figures are illustrative and do not represent any real company.
